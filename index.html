Our Story

A Local Favourite
Since Day One

What started at our flagship Marcos branch has grown into 3 beloved locations across Baguio City. From Bakakeng Central to Tam-awan and Legarda, Dang's BBQ & Inihaws has become one of Baguio's most-loved neighbourhood BBQ spots. We keep it simple: fresh meat, real charcoal heat, and a recipe that tastes like home.

Our menu celebrates the heart of Filipino inihaw culture — grilled chicken, pork, and seafood served at prices that let you eat like a king without breaking the bank. Pull up a seat at any of our branches, enjoy the mountain air, and taste what locals have been raving about.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our locations, menu, and hours.

Dang's BBQ & Inihaws has 3 branches in Baguio City: the main Marcos branch on Marcos Hi-Way in Bakakeng Central (beside Hollywood Inn exit gate), the Tam-awan branch at BASTI'S Transient House Purok 1, and the Legarda branch at 30 General Lim Street.
Dang's BBQ is very affordable. Single skewers start from ₱10 (chicken isaw, pork balat), individual grilled items range from ₱30 to ₱120, and combo meals with rice are ₱150 to ₱200.
Yes, all 3 branches of Dang's BBQ & Inihaws offer dine-in and takeout. It's perfect for groups, picnics, and family gatherings.
The Marcos branch (main) is open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM. The Tam-awan branch is open Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM. For the Legarda branch, please call ahead at 0926 654 5119 to confirm hours.
Dang's Chicken Inasal w/ Rice (₱150) is a customer favourite, along with the Pork BBQ skewers (₱40) and Chicken Kebab (₱95). All meats are grilled fresh over real charcoal.
