Our Story

A Local Favourite
Since Day One

Nestled along Marcos Hi-Way in Bakakeng Central, right beside the exit gate of Hollywood Inn, Dang's BBQ & Inihaws has become one of Baguio's most-loved neighbourhood BBQ spots. We keep it simple: fresh meat, real charcoal heat, and a recipe that tastes like home.

Our menu celebrates the heart of Filipino inihaw culture — grilled chicken, pork, and seafood served at prices that let you eat like a king without breaking the bank. Pull up a seat, enjoy the mountain air, and taste what locals have been raving about.
